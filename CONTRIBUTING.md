# Contributing

Contributions should be small, reviewable, provider-neutral, and covered by an appropriate tabletop or executable conformance case.

By contributing, you certify the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/) using a `Signed-off-by` trailer. Do not contribute private project history, credentials, customer data, operational evidence, or third-party material without a compatible license and attribution.

Contract changes require:

1. a stated compatibility impact;
2. migration and rollback notes;
3. an updated conformance case;
4. independent review.
